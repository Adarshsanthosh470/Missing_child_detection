About the Project

Trackwise is an AI-powered platform specifically engineered to address the critical societal challenges of missing children and human trafficking. The system aims to transform traditional investigation methods by replacing manual, fragmented processes with intelligent automation and real-time detection capabilities.

Thousands of children go missing annually due to abductions or displacements. Success rates for tracing victims are low due to fragmented data and manual investigations. Trackwise aims to prevent child trafficking through intelligent automation and real-time detection.
